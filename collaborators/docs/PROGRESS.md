# Research stages and current position

[Briefing](../README.md) · [Scientific results](RESULTS.md) · [Rules experiments](RULES.md)

**Current step: the specified analyses and component pilot have been completed, scored and reported. We are presenting the results for scientific discussion—not waiting for unblinding and not automatically starting another protein or experiment.** This page describes the September 2026 delivery; it is not a live monitor of jobs on another machine.

## Why the stages matter

The original goal has four priorities: identify reliable scientific quantities; compare heterogeneous evidence; make useful analyses reusable; then test whether limited AI automation helps. The last two serve the first two. A working tool interface, a successful replay or a large rule table is not a substitute for a supported answer.

Meeting basis: [28 July decisions](../../review/pro_briefing_20260910/meetings/20260728_DECISIONS_ZH.md) and [24 August decisions](../../review/pro_briefing_20260910/meetings/20260824_DECISIONS_ZH.md). These are organized meeting records, not new independent validation.

## What is actually complete

The latest campaign produced **74 final outputs: 72 controlled-comparison outputs plus two plain-Agent ADK outputs**. The feedback analysis additionally preserved and scored **24 initial submissions**. Final scores were sealed before condition labels were revealed. Those labels are now public; the campaign is finished. [Unblinding record](../../review/four-layer-20260910/outputs/UNBLINDING.json), [individual scores](../../review/four-layer-20260910/outputs/UNBLINDED_SCORES.csv).

The current campaign cost was **$1.20292179**. Four reconciled earlier batches cost **$0.28550861**, making **$1.48843040 for those listed batches**. This is not the project's complete historical cost, and preparation or correction time was not measured as a saved-human-time benefit. [Work and cost ledger](../../review/four-layer-20260910/outputs/WORK_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.json).

The public reading pages and offline numerical replay are also delivered. The replay checks selected analysed-table results; it does not rebuild raw coordinates or reevaluate the Agent answers. Independent domain-expert assessment of the scientific interpretation and answer scoring remains outstanding.

## What the results do not establish

The scorer also helped prepare the cases. Most sources had been exposed during development; there was one new ADK source group and four model runs per question–condition pair. Multiple runs on one problem describe variability, not additional independent proteins. No overall scientific-accuracy percentage or universal component ranking follows from this pilot.

The portable reproduction and the component experiment also have different purposes. The former asks whether the included table summaries can be regenerated. The latter asks how particular aids changed Agent answers. Neither alone demonstrates autonomous discovery of a correct scientific workflow from raw papers and trajectories.

## Next decision—not a new execution plan

**Working recommendation:** retain the source-linked scientific knowledge and usable analyses; pause expansion of the current rule selector and generic conclusion checker. Ordinary tool-assisted analysis is an acceptable outcome when the added machinery has not shown a clear benefit.

The most useful discussion is which unresolved scientific comparison would change an interpretation. In HSP90, the relationship between native NOE tolerances and structural-state meaning is a concrete example. Another case should be selected for its question and evidence, not merely because its protein name is next on an old list.

No new experiments, downloads, runtime changes or automatic stage transitions are authorized by this page. A future proposal should change one specific capability, use a suitable comparison and have an endpoint. It should not keep repairing these same exposed questions until one architecture appears to win.
