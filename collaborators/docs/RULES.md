# What the Rules Table contributes to the research

The Rules Table is a source-linked record of scientific prerequisites and limits. It contains 33 candidate rules derived from 11 papers. Its useful content is the relationship between a question, the evidence needed, an applicable method and the strongest supported conclusion.

The working analysis protocol asks seven questions:

1. What scientific quantity is being requested: geometry, population, kinetics, an observable or function?
2. Do the inputs describe compatible molecular objects and conditions?
3. What does each measurement actually observe?
4. What statistical unit and time interpretation does the data support?
5. Was evidence used for fitting, calibration or independent validation?
6. Is an appropriate forward model available between structures and observables?
7. Which alternatives remain unresolved, and what analysis could distinguish them?

These questions help communicate assumptions. They do not replace established numerical methods or domain judgment.

## What the component tests established

The completed pilot produced mixed case-dependent results. Retrieved method cards did not consistently outperform the short protocol. Full selected rules performed better on the Q05 nanodisc question but worse on HSP90. Explicit subquestions improved supported coverage in both tested cases, although HSP90 had one additional core error across four runs. The current one-round conclusion checker did not establish useful correction.

The practical implication is to retain the scientific protocol and literature knowledge while requiring additional evidence before expanding automated rule selection or enforcement. Most evaluated cases were exposed development materials; one new source group and four runs per condition do not establish general effectiveness. The scorer also curated the cases, so independent domain review remains outstanding.

This group package presents the research meaning of those results. Internal prompts, provider exchanges and execution-management records are omitted.
