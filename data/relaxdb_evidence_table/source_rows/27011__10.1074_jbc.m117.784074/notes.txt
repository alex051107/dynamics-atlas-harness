Discrepancies and notes:
1. Paper vs BMRB 27011 deposit card (missing pH and buffer in paper): Neither the main text nor the supplemental PDF reports sample pH or buffer composition—only temperature (25 °C), protein concentrations (0.8 mM 13C,15N-labeled or 0.6 mM 15N-labeled), and 5% polyacrylamide gel for RDC alignment. By contrast, the BMRB 27011 card specifies 298.2 K (= 25 °C), pH 6.6, 600/850/950 MHz, and "sigma1.1 [U-100% 15N] 0.6 mM; sodium chloride natural abundance 10 mM; sodium azide natural abundance 6 mM; phosphate buffer natural abundance 20 mM". Note also that BMRB 27011 lists sequence_length = 73, corresponding to Ala1-Asp71 plus the two XhoI cloning residues Leu72-Glu73 preceding the C-terminal His6 tag (p. 11615; Fig. 2/3 sequence header also shows LEHHHH...). Chemical shifts for structure determination were deposited separately in BMRB 34089 (PDB 5MWW).
2. Internal text vs Figure 2 panel/parameter discrepancies:
   - Results text (p. 11612) cites Fig. 2B for slow internal correlation time tau_s and Fig. 2C for Rex, whereas in Figure 2 panel B is tau_e (ps), panel C is tau_s (ns), and panel D is Rex (s^-1).
   - Figure 2B caption ("B, effective correlation time tau_e") writes "Standard deviations of tau_s of Ser45 was 334 ps. The values of the following residues, exceeding the plotted range, are not shown: Glu21, 1.2 ± 1.2 ns; Glu33, 0.8 ± 0.7 ns; Arg37, 0.7 ± 0.5 ns; and Asp46, 0.9 ± 0.2 ns." Writing "tau_s" for Ser45 inside the tau_e panel description appears to be a symbol typo for tau_e (334 ps exceeds the 0-100 ps y-axis of panel B).
