Discrepancies and notes:
1. Internal temperature discrepancy vs BMRB 26513 deposit card: Experimental Procedures (p. 34571) reports 15N relaxation (T1, T2, hetNOE) on 1.0 mM 15N-labeled MptpA in 150 mM NaCl, 50 mM HEPES (pH 7.0), 10 mM DTT at T = 298 K on a 600 MHz spectrometer—matching the BMRB 26513 card word-for-word (including sequence length 167 = subcloning G(-3)-G(0) + Rv2234 Met1-Ser163). However, Fig. 2C caption (p. 34574) states "backbone dynamics of MptpA measured at 600 MHz and T = 303 K" (303 K was the temperature of the 1.2 mM 50 mM Arg/Glu, 25 mM HEPES structure-determination sample deposited under BMRB 18533). Both temperatures are preserved with locators.
2. Pro127 listed under amide exchange broadening: On p. 34576, the authors state "residues Thr119-Ala121 as well as Glu125 and Pro127 located in the D-loop are not observable due to exchange broadening", although Pro127 lacks a backbone amide proton.
3. Phosphate formula typo and residue-name typos in text:
   - On p. 34576 ("Interaction with Phosphate Ions"), inorganic phosphate (Pi) is printed once as "20 mM NaH2PO3" (phosphite formula).
   - Residue 89 is Leu89 in the sequence/structure (beta3 Leu85-Leu89, p. 34572; PtkA CSP list Leu89, p. 34577) but printed twice as "Lys89" on p. 34576.
   - W-loop boundaries are given as Gly44-Asp55 (p. 34572) vs Ser44-Glu55 (Fig. 2B caption, p. 34574).
   - D-loop boundaries are given as Arg111-Asp131 (p. 34572, Fig. 2B) vs Arg111-Gly131 (p. 34576), and the superimposed D-loop segment as Leu122-Asp131 (p. 34574) vs Lys122-Asp131 (Table 2, p. 34575).
4. Unit of tau_c: On p. 34572, overall rotational correlation time in diffusion anisotropy refinement is printed as "tau_c = 12.12" without explicit "ns" unit.
