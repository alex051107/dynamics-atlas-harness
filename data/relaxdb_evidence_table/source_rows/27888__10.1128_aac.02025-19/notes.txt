4. Discrepancies and uncertainties:
- Sample concentration discrepancy for CPMG: Results (p. 5) and general Methods (p. 10) state inhibitor-bound experiments used 0.38 mM BlaC with 2 mM avibactam or 100 mM clavulanic acid, whereas the CPMG paragraph in Methods (p. 11) states "1.0, 0.8, or 0.4 mM 15N-enriched BlaC samples for free protein at 20 T and at 14 T or with 100 mM inhibitor at 20 T, respectively" (applying 100 mM inhibitor without distinguishing 2 mM avibactam from 100 mM clavulanic acid).
- Minimum R2,eff error discrepancy: Methods (p. 11) states CATIA grouped fits used a minimum R2,eff standard deviation of 0.8 s^-1, whereas Fig. S10 caption states a user-defined minimum of 0.3 s^-1 for the avibactam-bound fit.
- Deposit card vs relaxation sample concentration: BMRB 27888 card lists the 0.7 mM [U-100% 13C; U-100% 15N] assignment sample, whereas free-state spin relaxation used 0.38 mM 15N-enriched BlaC, free-state CPMG used 1.0 mM (20 T) and 0.8 mM (14 T) 15N-enriched BlaC, and CEST used 0.8 mM 15N-enriched BlaC (static field for CEST not specified between 600 and 850 MHz).
- Numbering: All residue numbers in the paper use the Ambler class A beta-lactamase scheme rather than 1-266 construct numbering (see Fig. S11).
