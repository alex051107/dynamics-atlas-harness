4. Discrepancies and uncertainties:
- Internal text contradiction on miR-16-1-D first titration point (1:0.05 protein:RNA): p. 1044 states peaks for 34L, 39G, 42I and 74T, 75G, 77G broadened to invisibility after the first addition of miR-16-1-D, whereas p. 1045 states "In the case of miR-16-1-D, the broadening did not appear for any residue at 1:0.05 ratio" and that A25, G28, D49, T74, G75, and G77 broadened at 1:0.1 (recorded in r6 caveat_note without picking sides).
- Supplement sequence typo in Table S3: The passenger strand printed for miR-16-1-B (5'-CCAGUAUUAACGUGCUGCUGAA-3') is identical to miR-16-1-M (missing the bulged U and A-to-U substitution shown for miR-16-1-B in main-text Fig. 1B).
- Minor text swap on p. 1045: "residues F62 and K80 ... belong to the a2 helix and b2 strand, respectively" reverses the secondary-structure order (F62 is in beta2 and K80 is in alpha2).
- Supplement footnote typo in Tables S1 and S2: Both footnotes state excluded structures were omitted from "supplementary figure S17", whereas the residue-pair distance histograms are in Figure S21 (Figure S17 is apo CPMG).
- Paper vs deposit card details: Paper states construct TRBP2-dsRBD1 (1-105 aa) and does not mention D2O %, whereas BMRB 50233 card lists sequence_length 108 and 10% D2O, and lists 600/750 MHz (apo CPMG used 600 and 750 MHz; R1/R2/nOe and HARD used 600 MHz).
