适用方法族：smFRET
要核对：在 G1>G2>G3>G5 的重点检查中要求 conformational-sampling component, photon shot-noise model, timescale/window, package/version, input trajectory and implementation receipt；若无法验证则走 NOISE_CONFORMATION_OR_IMPLEMENTATION_UNRESOLVED。
方法/算子：noise_and_conformation_decomposition

来源：Steffen 2021 FRETraj；STF-S02; STF-S03
迁移范围：predicted width is not protein dynamics without the decomposition
