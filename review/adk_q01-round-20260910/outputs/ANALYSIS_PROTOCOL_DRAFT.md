# ADK source admission and descriptive analysis protocol (before results)

Scientific task: compare domain geometry in the two deposited trajectories as single-source MD. Experimental 4.3ms inference is author context, not the timescale of the stored simulations. Source Fig1/4 describe LID118–167, NMP30–67, CORE1–29/68–117/161–214. The source region definitions overlap at161–167; SI S5 also gives CORE68–115. Do not silently treat the regions as disjoint or claim exact reproduction of an author-defined scalar.

Use a transparent descriptive scalar: mean of C-alpha pair distances between NMP30–67 and unambiguous CORE residues1–29/68–115/168–214; and LID118–160 versus that CORE. These intentionally omit disputed boundary residues, use the same fixed membership for both trajectories, and are project descriptive summaries of the paper's distance-matrix idea, not a newly inferred biological state. Native all214 C-alpha coordinates remain available locally for checking. This choice precedes results and will not be tuned to produce a particular direction.

Reconstruct consecutive C-alpha positions with minimum-image chain increments in the measured orthorhombic cell. Confirm sequence, all214 C-alpha atoms, neighbor distances, cells/angles, XTC times, nm-to-A conversion and matching atom counts. Inspect maximum of every residue-pair distance contributing to the two displayed summary scalars against half the minimum cell length; a breach triggers the adopted physical gate, even if scientifically explainable, before freezing. Raw coordinate tables never enter common.

Report native sampling/window; compare first and last10% of each trajectory (same proportion, actual windows shown), all-frame distributions and shared-time-window sensitivity. No fitted thresholds, equilibrium populations, transition rates or causal ATP-binding time. Distance changes measure domain separation, not full conformation or directly observed binding. Different starting structures are not independent experimental replicates.

This is a preparation error, not a defect in the deposited dataset.
