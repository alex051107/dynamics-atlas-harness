1. **Protein** — Adenylate kinase (Adk), *Geobacillus stearothermophilus* (older refs: *Bacillus stearothermophilus*) [paper, Methods]. UniProt: not stated.

2. **Construct and conditions** — Full-length Adk, C-terminal TEV/His6-tag, no point mutations reported [paper, Methods]. Native tetra-Cys "zinc site" in the ATP lid held Zn2+ (diamagnetic reference) or Co2+ (paramagnetic, PCS sample) [paper, Results]. Buffer pH 7.0 (MOPS/NaCl/TCEP); CPMG/PCS at 25 °C, 600 MHz [paper, Methods]. Three conditions compared: "turnover" (20 mM MgCl2 + 20 mM ADP), "no-Mg2+" (20 mM ADP only), "apo" (no additions) [paper, Fig. 1c; Extended Data Fig. 2–4].

3. **Apo relative to** — Mg2+-ADP, standing in for Adk's substrates/products (ATP, AMP, ADP) [paper, Extended Data Fig. 2c, 4a–c: "apo" = no nucleotide, no Mg2+]. Used only as a qualitative comparison, not as the source of exchange or structure data [our reading of the paper's data].

4. **Verdict** — Not included. The only state pair with measured exchange rate, population and a solved structure was characterized under nucleotide-saturating "turnover" conditions; since [ADP] (20 mM) far exceeds KM,ADP (~50 µM), the authors state the high-energy state remains substrate/product-occupied (Results) — neither member of the pair is apo. The true apo sample gets only a static PCS/linewidth comparison; no exchange rate, population or structure determined [paper + inference].

5. **Major state** — Under turnover, the dominant species is closed, approximating the Ap5A-bound crystal structure (PDB 4QBH) [paper, Results]. Population implied ≈87.4% (100% − 12.6%) [our reading of the paper's data].

6. **Alternative state(s)** — A partially open "high-energy" state solved de novo from PCS restraints: AMP lid opens ~15° (~50% of full range), Co2+ in the ATP lid shifts ~1.8 Å; closer to closed (r.m.s.d. 2.67 Å) than to open (r.m.s.d. 7.03 Å, PDB 4AKE, cross-species) [paper, Fig. 3c–d]. Still substrate/product-occupied, not apo. The true apo sample separately shows line broadening and a poor fit to the closed structure (Q = 47.9%), consistent with a more open average state, but no discrete alternative apo state is proposed [paper, Extended Data Fig. 2c, 4a–c].

7. **Evidence type** — Paramagnetic-enhanced 1HN CPMG relaxation dispersion (Co2+ vs Zn2+ Adk), globally fit to the Carver–Richards two-state equation; PCS-restrained structure calculation (expectation-maximization simulated annealing, XPLOR-NIH) resolving sign/magnitude ambiguity in minor-state PCS [paper, Results; Methods]. Apo condition: static HSQC/PCS comparison only, no CPMG reported [paper, Extended Data Fig. 4c].

8. **Exchange parameters** — Turnover: kex = 1,428 ± 83 s⁻¹ (global Zn2++Co2+ fit); minor population pB = 12.6 ± 2.5%; kopen = 180 ± 36 s⁻¹ [paper, Results; Supplementary Fig. 1]. Without Mg2+ (ADP only, still not apo): kopen ≈ 2.6 ± 0.3 s⁻¹, slow exchange [paper, Extended Data Fig. 3c]. 93/186 assigned residues gave usable PCS [paper, Results]. Δδ range not stated (only ">0.1 ppm for many residues," apo-vs-turnover); per-residue values are in Supplementary Table 1 [not stated].

9. **Residues or regions** — AMP lid (32–55, largest changes) and ATP lid (128–157, smaller, metal-proximal); hinge/core also separate rigid bodies [paper, Methods; Fig. 3b,d]. Apo broadening/PCS loss is described as widespread, not localized [paper, Extended Data Fig. 4a–c].

10. **Identity basis** — The turnover minor state was solved de novo as a 3D structure from ambiguous PCS restraints, not assigned by shift-matching, then compared to closed/open crystal structures [paper, Results]. Method validated on 12 synthetic Adk conformers plus leave-one-out cross-validation [paper, Results]. No identity basis exists for a discrete apo alternative state, because none is proposed [inference].

11. **Structural mapping and simulations** — Mapped structures: closed PDB 4QBH, used directly [paper, Results]; open PDB 4AKE, cross-species, homology-modeled onto G. stearothermophilus for benchmarking [paper, Methods]. Minor-state coordinates: not deposited, available on request [paper, "Data availability"]. No new MD/metadynamics was run here; an external metadynamics study (ref. 43) and the authors' earlier high-pressure-NMR paper (ref. 44) are cited only as consistent [paper, Discussion].

12. **Functional relevance** — Proposed, not newly tested: the state is argued to open a release path for the AMP-lid nucleotide and prime the pocket for the next substrate (conformational selection, then induced fit) [paper, Fig. 3e,f]. kopen (180 ± 36 s⁻¹) is similar order to the previously reported kcat ≈ 100 s⁻¹ (cited, not re-measured here) [paper, intro]. No new mutant, binding or activity assay is presented for this state [not stated].

13. **Competing explanations addressed** — Full lid-opening, previously proposed from smFRET (refs. 27, 32) and the group's earlier chemical-shift-only study (ref. 25), is ruled out: the ATP-lid metal moves only ~1.8 Å [paper, Discussion]. Paramagnet/tag motion as an artifact is ruled out by matching PCS- and RDC-derived tensors [paper, Extended Data Fig. 2d]. A metal-identity artifact is addressed by Zn2+/Co2+ kex agreeing within error, both attributed to lid opening, not independent metal motion [paper, Results].

14. **What this evidence cannot support** — Cannot support any claim about the structure, population or exchange rate of a ligand-free (apo) alternative state — none was measured with CPMG here. Cannot treat the "high-energy" state as apo; it remains substrate/product-occupied. Cannot extend to ATP or AMP directly (only Mg2+-ADP tested) or to other Adk orthologs (4AKE needed cross-species homology modeling, benchmarking only). Cannot link opening-rate or population to catalytic rate across variants (none tested). Minor-state coordinates are not publicly deposited, limiting independent verification [inference].

15. **Provenance** — DOI 10.1038/s41586-022-04468-9; PMC9126080. Fig. 1, 3; Extended Data Fig. 1–4; Suppl. Table 1, Suppl. Fig. 1 (not accessed). Methods (expression/purification, NMR spectroscopy, data availability). BMRB 51232 (Zn2+), 51233 (Co2+) [paper]. PDB 4QBH (closed, ref. 26), PDB 4AKE (open/benchmark, ref. 68) [paper].
