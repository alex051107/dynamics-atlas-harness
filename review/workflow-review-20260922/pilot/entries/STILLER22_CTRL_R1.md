1. **Protein** — Adenylate kinase (Adk), *Geobacillus stearothermophilus*. UniProt: not stated [paper].

2. **Construct and conditions** — Native sequence, C-terminal TEV+His6 tag, BL21(DE3); no Adk point mutations. Natural tetra-Cys site (ATP lid) holds diamagnetic Zn2+ or paramagnetic Co2+ (similar ionic radii give equivalent structure/function) [paper, Methods "Protein expression and purification"]. NMR: 2 mM protein, 50 mM MOPS, 50 mM NaCl, 2 mM TCEP, 0.02% NaN3, pH 7.0; assignment at 40 °C (92%/83%, Zn2+/Co2+). Dynamics data below: "turnover" (20 mM MgCl2 + 20 mM ADP), 25 °C, single field, Agilent DD2 600 MHz [paper, Methods "NMR spectroscopy"].

3. **Apo relative to** — Nucleotide substrate (paper's "apo" sample has no added nucleotide; ADP is the ligand used here; native reaction is ATP + AMP ⇌ 2 ADP) [paper, ED Figs. 2, 4]. Neither state below is actually ligand-free — see Verdict.

4. **Verdict** — Not included: the closed⇌partially-open process solved here was measured only under saturating Mg2+-ADP; the paper states the minor state itself "must be occupied with substrate or product" — both states are nucleotide-bound, not apo [paper, Results "Structure of the high-energy state of Adk" para 2].

5. **Major state** — Fully closed, ADP-bound conformation; PCS under Mg2+-ADP fits the closed crystal structure at Q = 11.6% (population-weighted) or Q = 7.6% under ADP-only slow exchange (purer closed read) [paper, Results para 2; ED Fig. 2a,b]. Population ≈ 87.4% (100% − pB) [our reading of the paper's data]. Structure: PDB 4QBH, Ap5A-bound closed Adk [paper, same section].

6. **Alternative state(s)** — Partially open state: AMP lid opens ~15° (~50% of closed→open transition); ATP-lid/Co2+ shifts ~1.8 Å, partly separating from bound nucleotide; closer to closed (r.m.s.d. 2.67 Å) than open (7.03 Å); still nucleotide-occupied [paper, Results para 2, Fig. 3c,d]. pB = 12.6 ± 2.5%. Solved de novo (PCS–CPMG/expectation-maximization + XPLOR-NIH); no PDB code given (Data availability lists only BMRB codes) — not stated/not deposited [paper, Data availability statement].

7. **Evidence type** — PCS-enhanced 1HN amide CPMG dispersion on paired Zn2+(dia)/Co2+(para) samples under turnover; corroborated by static [1H-15N]-HSQC/PCS comparison across ligand states (Mg2+-ADP, ADP, apo); minor-state structure from a stochastic expectation-maximization/simulated-annealing algorithm in XPLOR-NIH [paper, Results "PCS–CPMG of Adk..."; Methods "Simulated annealing protocol"].

8. **Exchange parameters** — Global fit: kex = 1,428 ± 83 s⁻¹, pB = 12.6 ± 2.5% (Zn2+ 1,355 ± 65 s⁻¹; Co2+ 1,367 ± 71 s⁻¹) [paper, Results para 3]. kopen = 180 ± 36 s⁻¹ (Mg2+-ADP) vs ≈2.6 ± 0.3 s⁻¹ (ADP alone) [paper, same section; ED Fig. 3c]. No single Δδ range stated; 103/186 residues show R2,ex > 3 Hz; paramagnetic Δδ is smaller than the ≥0.5 ppm predicted between open/closed crystal structures [paper, para 3–4; ED Fig. 4g].

9. **Residues or regions** — Global signal (103/186 residues, R2,ex > 3 Hz); largest change in the AMP lid and core near the metal site, secondary in the ATP lid. Refinement domains: core (1–28, 62–113, 165–217), AMP lid (32–55), hinge (116–125), ATP lid (128–157) [paper, Methods, simulated-annealing section].

10. **Identity basis** — Not chemical-shift matching to a known reference; identity comes from de novo PCS-restrained rigid-body calculation, benchmarked on 12 Adk crystal structures (open→closed; correct PCS assignment, 90 ± 8% of residues), classified by r.m.s.d. to closed (2.67 Å) vs open (7.03 Å) [paper, Results, benchmarking section and high-energy-state section].

11. **Structural mapping and simulations** — Reference structures: closed PDB 4QBH, open PDB 4AKE. Twelve further Adk structures (open→closed) only benchmarked the method, not a target [paper, Methods "Benchmarking PCS–CPMG with computer-generated data"]. No MD simulation of Adk was run; a metadynamics study (ref. 43) is cited as external support [paper, Discussion].

12. **Functional relevance** — Proposed cycle (Fig. 3f): apo ensemble → partial closing to this state → nucleotide binds (conformational selection) → full closure (induced fit) → phosphoryl transfer → rate-limiting opening (kopen = 180 ± 36 s⁻¹) → product release; opened AMP lid reveals a tunnel for product escape [paper, Results para 3, Fig. 3e,f]. This apo-binding role is a proposed model, not measured on an apo sample — see field 14.

13. **Competing explanations addressed** — Revises the group's earlier chemical-shift model of fuller bilateral lid opening (ref. 25) and smFRET reports of large ATP-lid movement (refs. 27, 32); Co2+-position data show only ~1.8 Å motion [paper, Discussion para 2]. Zn2+/Co2+ reporters give statistically indistinguishable kex; PCS vs paramagnetic-RDC tensors agree, against tag-mobility artifacts [paper, ED Fig. 2d]; annealing converges identically from open or closed starts [paper, ED Fig. 6].

14. **What this evidence cannot support** — Cannot resolve the true ligand-free apo enzyme's conformation: apo data are only qualitative (broadened HSQC/PCS, Q = 47.9% fit to closed state), with no exchange rate, population or structure determined [paper, ED Figs. 2c, 4]. Rigid-body refinement cannot report intradomain distortion. Single field (600 MHz) and temperature (25 °C): no independent field-dependence check. No new mutational or binding assay tests the proposed mechanism [paper, Methods, NMR section].
