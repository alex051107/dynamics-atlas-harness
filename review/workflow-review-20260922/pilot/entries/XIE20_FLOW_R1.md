1. **Protein** — Abl (ABL1/c-Abl) kinase domain. Species not stated as "human" in the paper's prose but implied by the Bcr-Abl/CML/imatinib-resistance framing and "Abl isoform 1b" numbering [paper, Introduction; Methods]; confirmed by PDB 6XR6 (Homo sapiens, UniProt P00519 via SIFTS) [our reading of the paper's data, PDB 6XR6]. UniProt ID not given in the paper text.

2. **Construct and conditions** — Kinase domain "Abl" = residues 248–534 of isoform 1b; "AblFK" = residues 1–534 or 83–534 (SH3-SH2-kinase); "AblFKΔSH3" = residues 138–534 [paper, Methods]. Buffer: 25 mM sodium phosphate pH 7.1, 75 mM NaCl, 3.0 mM BME, D2O [paper, Methods]. Unliganded spectra at 10 °C (283 K) [paper, Methods]; deposited structures: pH 7.1 (G), 6.5 (I1, mutant), 7.7 (I2, mutant), all 283 K [our reading of the paper's data, BMRB 30770/30771/30772]. 1.1 GHz–600 MHz Bruker, cryoprobes [paper, Methods].

3. **Apo relative to** — No nucleotide (ATP/ADP/Mg2+) or small molecule bound. The paper frames both alternative states against two ATP-competitive inhibitors, comparing "apo and PD173955-bound" and "apo and imatinib-bound" shifts [paper, Fig. 1 legend, E–F].

4. **Verdict** — Strong. CEST exchange data plus three independent lines (mutant panel with ITC/activity readouts, de novo NOE structures of both excited states, matching inhibitor-bound reference structures); numbers traceable throughout.

5. **Major state** — Ground state (G), population 88% [paper, Fig. 1D; Methods]; catalytically active (DFG-in, αC-in, A-loop open) [paper, Fig. 2A]. PDB 6XR6/BMRB 30770.

6. **Alternative state(s)** — I1(E1): population 5.9±0.1% ("6%") [paper, Fig. 1D/Methods]; DFG-out, αC-in, A-loop open; close to the PD173955-bound complex [paper, Fig. 2B]. PDB 6XR7/BMRB 30771. I2(E2): population 6.1±0.7% ("6%"; elsewhere "5%" for the T408Y baseline) [paper, Fig. 1D; "excited state 2" section]; DFG-out, αC-out, A-loop closed, P-loop rotated; resembles but is not identical to the imatinib complex [paper, Fig. 2C–D]. PDB 6XRG/BMRB 30772.

7. **Evidence type** — 13C-methyl CEST, 104 probes [paper, Fig. 1B]; 1H-13C–correlated spectra integrating slow-exchange peaks above ~5% population (population-shifted mutants; AblFK, unstable for CEST) [paper, Methods]; magnetization-exchange cross-peaks linking AblT408Y peak sets [paper, fig. S6C]; NOE-based structure determination, CYANA/CNS [paper, Methods]; ITC and CrkII kinase assays [paper, Methods].

8. **Exchange parameters** — Global 3-state linear fit (favored model): kex(G↔E1)=46.8±4.3 s⁻¹, kex(E1↔E2)=88.7±13.5 s⁻¹, pE1=5.9±0.1%, pE2=6.1±0.7% [paper, Methods]. Two-state control fit on AblH415P (I2 essentially absent): kex=46.8±4.3 s⁻¹, pI1=12.1±0.5%, χred²=1.38 [paper, Methods]. CEST-detectable window used: ~20–500 s⁻¹ [paper, main text]. No single Δϖ range given; example Leu406: CD1≈−2.8, CD2≈+2.1 ppm (13C) [paper, Fig. 1F legend].

9. **Residues or regions** — 83/104 methyl probes show CEST exchange (40 one dip, 43 two dips), concentrated in/near the activation loop and αC helix but spread through the domain [paper, Fig. 1B, legend].

10. **Identity basis** — Two lines per state. I1: (i) CEST Δϖ(E1−G) correlates linearly with the apo-minus-PD173955 shift difference, excluding methyls within 6 Å of inhibitor [paper, Fig. 1E]; (ii) NOE-solved structure (AblM309L/H415P) matches the PD173955 co-crystal in A-loop/αC helix [paper, Methods]. I2: (i) CEST Δϖ(E2−G) correlates with the apo-minus-imatinib shift difference; αC-helix outliers explained as genuine further rearrangement on binding [paper, Fig. 1F; fig. S8]; (ii) NOE-solved structure (I2M variant) matches imatinib's A-loop/DFG but differs in αC helix/P-loop [paper, Fig. 2D]. Full 3D structures were solved for both, not shift-based models alone, so identity is stated directly; the imatinib match is flagged partial.

11. **Structural mapping and simulations** — All three states have deposited NMR structures (PDB 6XR6/6XR7/6XRG) [paper, Data availability]. I1/I2 were solved on mutant backgrounds at pH 6.5 and 7.7 vs. pH 7.1 for WT [our reading of the paper's data, BMRB entries]. No MD simulation described in text/Methods (SI PDF not opened); prior external Abl MD/Markov-state work is cited (ref. 15), not compared to these NMR data.

12. **Functional relevance** — I2: selectively binds imatinib (structural match); ITC affinity measured for WT and 4 resistance mutants (distinct mechanisms) — H415P (5-fold weaker, I2<1%), Y272H (~10-fold weaker, I2 ~7-fold down), E274V (weaker; I2 down ~1.4 kcal/mol in I2M background), F378V (~4-fold weaker, I2 ~4-fold down) [paper, Fig. 3C–G; fig. S9]. Kinase activity (CrkII phosphorylation): AblF401V (I2>95%) ≥20-fold less active than WT; AblM309L/L320I (pI2≈82%) essentially inactive [paper, Fig. 4B,E; fig. S11]. pTyr412 phosphorylation, gatekeeper T334I both deplete I2, stabilize active state [paper, Fig. 4D,F]. I1: relevance is resemblance to the PD173955 complex only — paper states significance "is not apparent"; no assay isolates I1 [paper, Discussion].

13. **Competing explanations addressed** — Methyls within 6 Å of inhibitor excluded from shift-correlation, separating ligand-contact shifts from state identity [paper, Fig. 1E–F legend]. Three exchange-topology models compared by χred² and consistency with the AblH415P two-state fit before selecting linear G↔E1↔E2 [paper, Methods]. Opposite pH-dependence of I1 vs. I2, plus the I2-eliminating H415P mutant, assign which CEST dip belongs to which state [paper, Methods; Fig. 6F].

14. **What this evidence cannot support** — That I1 has demonstrated function beyond resembling one inhibitor complex. That I1/I2 coordinates are wild-type structures rather than mutant-stabilized proxies validated by shift-matching. Any in-cell, myristoylated or full-length population/activity claim — measurements use E. coli-expressed, unmyristoylated fragments in one buffer; T334I-in-myristoylated-Abl activation is a literature comparison (refs. 62,69), not new data. Single-variable causal isolation for any resistance mutant — each rests on structural rationale plus correlation between NMR population shift and a separate ITC/activity readout.
