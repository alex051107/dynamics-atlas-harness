1. **Protein** — Abl (Abelson) tyrosine kinase, kinase domain (construct "Abl," residues 248–534, from full-length Abl isoform 1b) [paper, Methods "Expression and preparation of proteins"]. Species not stated; the Bcr-Abl/CML/imatinib-resistance framing throughout implies human ABL1 [inference, Results section on resistance mutations]. UniProt ID not given.

2. **Construct and conditions** — Main construct: isolated kinase domain "Abl" (248–534); also "AblFK" (SH3-SH2-kinase, residues 1–534 or 83–534) and "AblFKΔSH3" (138–534) [Methods]. ~30 point/multi-site substitutions shift state populations (e.g. M309L, H415P, T408Y) [Methods]. No nucleotide, cofactor or inhibitor in the CEST/structure samples (apo); 25 mM sodium phosphate pH 7.1, 75 mM NaCl, 3 mM BME (TCEP for ITC), D2O; 10 °C (complexes 10–20 °C); pH varied 6.5–7.7; fields 600 MHz–1.1 GHz, cryoprobes [Methods; Fig. 6D–F].

3. **Apo relative to** — ATP-competitive Abl inhibitors, chiefly imatinib (and PD173955), and ATP/Mg²⁺; none present in the samples used for the ground/I1/I2 exchange data [paper, Methods "NMR Sample Preparation"].

4. **Verdict** — Strong. Quantitative CEST data (rate, population, per-residue Δω) for two apo excited states, corroborated by 2D ¹H-¹³C spectra of population-shifted mutants, independent structure determination, and functional data (ITC, kinase assays, patient mutations), all traceable to the paper.

5. **Major state** — Ground/active state, population 88% in the isolated kinase domain [Fig. 1D]; DFG-in, αC-in, A-loop open — canonical active architecture [Fig. 2A]. Solved by solution NMR (PDB 6XR6, BMRB 30770) [Data availability].

6. **Alternative state(s)** — I1 (E1), ≈6%: DFG flipped 180° ("out"; Asp400/Phe401 swap), A-loop open but backbone-flipped, αC-in; resembles the PD173955-bound complex. Solved using AblM309L/H415P (population ~50%); PDB 6XR7, BMRB 30771 [Fig. 1C–E, 2B]. I2 (E2), ≈6% (34% in AblFK; 95% with GNF5): DFG-out, A-loop closed, translating ~35 Å to mask the substrate site, αC-out, P-loop rotated; resembles the imatinib complex except αC/P-loop. Solved using AblM309L/T408Y and AblG269E/M309L/T408Y ("I2M," 85–90%); PDB 6XRG, BMRB 30772 [Fig. 1C,D,F, 2C].

7. **Evidence type** — ¹³C-methyl CEST on 104 probes, fit with ChemEx/Bloch-McConnell; corroborating 2D ¹H-¹³C HMQC directly resolving both states in shifted mutants; a magnetization-exchange experiment linking the peak sets (fig. S6C); ITC; CrkII-phosphorylation kinase assay; NOE-restrained structure determination (CYANA/CNS) [Methods].

8. **Exchange parameters** — kex(G↔I1)=46.8±4.3 s⁻¹, kex(I1↔I2)=88.7±13.5 s⁻¹, linear three-state model [Fig. 1D; Methods]. Populations: pG=88%, pI1≈5.9±0.1%, pI2≈6.1±0.7% (wild-type isolated kinase domain) [Fig. 1D; Methods]. No single Δω range is stated; representative values given, e.g. Leu406 Cδ1≈−2.8 p.p.m., Cδ2≈+2.1 p.p.m. (I2 vs. imatinib) [Fig. 1F]; full data are presumably in the supplement (not reviewed).

9. **Residues or regions** — 83/104 assayed methyls show exchange (40 one dip, 43 two dips), spread through the kinase domain, concentrated near the A-loop and αC helix [Fig. 1B]. Differing elements: DFG motif, A-loop (403–412, incl. Tyr412), αC helix (Glu305–Lys290; Glu311), P-loop (Tyr272), regulatory spine (Met309/Leu320/His380/Phe401), gatekeeper Thr334 [Figs. 2, 4A].

10. **Identity basis** — Two lines per state: (i) region-by-region correlation between CEST-derived Δω(excited−ground) and independently measured Δω(apo−inhibitor) — "remarkable similarity" for I1/PD173955, "excellent correlation" for I2/imatinib [Fig. 1E,F]; (ii) population-raising mutations that don't perturb other shifts yield NOE data giving explicit structures consistent with the CEST-predicted environment [Results]. E311K selectively destabilizes I1, not I2 (Fig. 2E), showing the two are independently modulable, not one artifact.

11. **Structural mapping and simulations** — All three states solved as NOE-restrained structures: ground=6XR6/30770, I1=6XR7/30771, I2=6XRG/30772 [Data availability]. I2 is also compared to the pre-existing Abl–imatinib crystal structure (PDB 1IEP, ref. 46) [Fig. 2D] and to inactive IRK/RTK structures (fig. S12, not reviewed). No MD tested I1/I2; ref. 15 (an Abl MD/Markov-state study) is cited only as background.

12. **Functional relevance** — I1 and I2 are pre-existing apo conformations that PD173955 and imatinib, respectively, selectively bind — conformational selection, not induced fit [Fig. 1E,F; 2D]. Seven patient imatinib-resistance mutations distal to the drug site (H415P, Y272H, E274V, F378V, G269E, Q271H, T231R) deplete pI2, matching ITC-measured affinity loss [Fig. 3C–G, S9]. SH3-SH2 docking raises pI2 six-fold (6%→34%; →95% with GNF5), the basis of Abl autoinhibition [Fig. 5A,B]. Gatekeeper T334I depletes I2 (quantified via AblI2M) and A-loop pTyr412 depletes both I1 and I2, each activating the kinase per CrkII-phosphorylation assays [Fig. 4C–F, S11].

13. **Competing explanations addressed** — CEST fits used only methyls with no peak overlap, a resolvable minor dip, and no confounding local dynamics [Methods]. Three three-state kinetic topologies were compared by χred² and checked against the independent AblH415P two-state fit (eliminates I2); populations >10% were excluded, inconsistent with profile shape and multi-day spectra showing no minor peaks [Methods]. Population-shifting mutations were confirmed not to add shift changes beyond those predicted from wild-type CEST data, arguing against artifact structures [Results]. A hydrophobicity-based explanation for the spine mutants' effect was tested and rejected [Results, near Fig. 4C].

14. **What this evidence cannot support** — I1's biological function: the paper states "the biological significance of the I1 state is not apparent" [Discussion]. I1/I2 structures come from population-enriching mutants, not the wild-type minor species directly — identity rests on shift-pattern matching, not a wild-type structure. The ~36 kcal/mol activation energy applies only to the ground↔I1 transition (via AblH415P), not I2 [Fig. 6G–I]. No cellular or in vivo data; only the isolated kinase domain and SH3-SH2 fragments were studied, so extrapolation to full-length or myristoylated Bcr-Abl in cells is untested.

15. **Provenance** — DOI 10.1126/science.abc2754; PMCID PMC7920495. Figures and Methods sections cited above; Table S1 (NOE restraints, not reviewed). PDB 6XR6/BMRB 30770 (active), 6XR7/30771 (I1), 6XRG/30772 (I2) [Data and materials availability]. Comparison structures cited: PDB 1IEP (ref. 46), 4TWP (ref. 48), 4XEY (ref. 83).
