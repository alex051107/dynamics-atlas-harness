1. **Protein** — NtrC (GlnG) receiver domain, NtrCR, of the bacterial NtrB–NtrC nitrogen-starvation two-component system [paper, Abstract; Intro §2]. Species Salmonella typhimurium [our reading: natural_source of BMRB 25125/25124, linked from cited PDB 2MSL/2MSK; not named in main text]. UniProt not stated; BMRB cross-references P41789 [our reading].

2. **Construct and conditions** — 124-residue domain, no mutation recorded [our reading, BMRB 25125 entity]. Two U-¹³C,¹⁵N samples: apo (sodium phosphate/NaCl) and BeF₃⁻-activated (+BeCl₂/NaF/MgCl₂ at Asp54); pH 6.75 [paper, Methods; salts/pH from BMRB 25124/25125, our reading]. 35 °C throughout; Varian 600 MHz (assignments/J-couplings), Bruker 800 MHz cryoprobe (NOESY) [paper, Methods].

3. **Apo relative to** — the phosphoryl group NtrB transfers to Asp54; probed as apo vs. BeF₃⁻ phosphomimic [paper, Intro §2; Methods].

4. **Verdict** — Weak. The alternative (active-like) conformer and its kinetics (~13,000 s⁻¹, ~15% population) are treated as established, load-bearing numbers, but cited from the authors' earlier work (refs 11–13), not measured here; new data are two NMR structures, targeted NOE comparisons and MD/MSM computation — no CPMG/CEST/R1ρ/ZZ-exchange experiment appears in Methods or in linked BMRB 25125's experiment list [paper, Methods; our reading of BMRB 25125].

5. **Major state** — Inactive conformation, dominant in apo (~85%, complement of the cited 15%) [inference]. Not one fixed structure: α4 well-formed centrally but only 20–30% helical at termini in the computed inactive macrostate, partial helicity spanning H84–Q96 [paper, Fig. 2F], corroborated by chemical-shift helical propensity on the apo sample [Fig. 5D]. New structure PDB 2MSL, apo, 35 °C, refined from 2428 NOE + 173 dihedral restraints [Table 1; Fig. 5A].

6. **Alternative state(s)** — Active-like conformation, minor apo species, ~15% [paper, Results, citing refs 12–13]. Structurally homogeneous in the model (~5% of 2168 MSM microstates) [paper, Results]. Matching structure PDB 2MSK — solved not from minor-population signal in the apo spectrum but from a separate BeF₃⁻-activated sample where it dominates [Methods; Fig. 5B].

7. **Evidence type** — No exchange-detecting experiment. New evidence: solution-NMR structure determination (assignment, NOESY, J-coupling side-chain dihedrals, TALOS+ dihedrals) on separate apo/BeF₃⁻ samples; chemical-shift helical-propensity analysis; targeted NOESY cross-peak comparison [paper, Methods; Fig. 5D–E]. Backed by string-method pathways, an unbiased-MD Markov State Model (Folding@home, ~1 ms cumulative), and long unbiased Anton MD [paper, Results/Methods]. Rate/population figures are cited from prior work, not reproduced [refs 11–13].

8. **Exchange parameters** — Rate ≈13,000 s⁻¹ [paper, Intro §2, citing refs 11–13; not remeasured]. Minor population ≈15% in apo [paper, Results, citing refs 12–13]. Chemical-shift-difference range: not stated (no dispersion/CEST fit). The paper's own non-equilibrium 2-macrostate MSM separately gives ≈52%/48%, flagged as imprecise [paper, Results]. MSM slow timescale ≈100 μs for I↔A; intra-I exchange up to ~10 μs [paper, Results].

9. **Residues or regions** — Helix α4, residues 82–101 (MSM clustering metric) [paper, Methods]. Y94, Q96, F99 reposition vs. helix α3/α5; A98 in an inactive-state hydrophobic patch; α4 N-terminal turn flexible even in A; Y101 orientation independent of T82, against T-Y coupling [paper, Results; Fig. 1A, 2A–B]. Asp54 is the trigger, not the reporter region [Intro].

10. **Identity basis** — Convergence of three lines, not direct exchange cross-peaks in apo spectra: (i) string/MSM computation predicts helix-4 rotation, with Y94/Q96 repositioning, as the I/A coordinate [paper, Results]; (ii) new BeF₃⁻ structure (2MSK) matches this computed active macrostate [Fig. 5B]; (iii) targeted NOESY cross-peaks confirm the switch — Y94–α3 only in apo data, Q96–α3 only in BeF₃⁻ data [Fig. 5E]. Both are restraint-refined structures, not shift-perturbation models, so "the structure" wording is used; the apo-minor-population-to-2MSK link itself stays computational/indirect.

11. **Structural mapping and simulations** — 2MSL (apo/inactive), 2MSK (BeF₃⁻/active), both new here [paper, Table 1]. Two independent, non-converging biased string pathways [Fig. 1]. MSM from 8000 unbiased Folding@home trajectories seeded along one string (2168 microstates, ~978 μs filtered): samples both macrostates, committor≈0.5 structures, and some trajectories complete full unbiased I↔A transitions [Fig. 2–3]. Long unbiased Anton MD (21–71 μs) per end-state samples I heterogeneity/A stability but no full transition [Fig. 4]. An independent MSM (Amber99SB, ref 42) agrees qualitatively, less I heterogeneity, attributed to force field [Fig. 6].

12. **Functional relevance** — Not independently tested; background/cited only. Activation is required for downstream transcriptional activation [paper, Intro §2, citing ref 15]; the barrier cost of losing nonnative H-bonds is cited from ref 13, not remeasured [Discussion]. Status: proposed/cited, not directly tested here.

13. **Competing explanations addressed** — T-Y coupling (Y101–T82): MSM shows Y101 untied to T82, agreeing with cited ref 25, which disproved that mechanism [Results]. String-method sampling artifact: cross-checked against this paper's unbiased MSM and an independently run MSM (ref 42); agreement argues against artifact [Fig. 6]. Revised F99 position vs. earlier NMR models: authors argue it does not undermine MSM conclusions, since F99 switching is not rate-limiting [Results].

14. **What this evidence cannot support** — Not primary evidence for the ~13,000 s⁻¹ rate or ~15% population: citations, not new measurements; no Δω or CEST/dispersion data given. Cannot show 2MSK is literally the minor species' structure inside the true apo ensemble — from a separate, population-shifted sample; the apo-minor-state link is computational, not observed exchange. MSM's 52%/48% and 5%/95% figures are model-internal, explicitly imprecise, not validated populations. No simulation captures a full spontaneous A↔I transition from one equilibrated end-state. No new functional or binding data.

PDB 2MSL (apo/inactive), 2MSK (BeF₃⁻/active) [paper, accession footnote]. BMRB 25125 (→2MSL), 25124 (→2MSK) [our reading, via RCSB cross-references; not printed in main text].
