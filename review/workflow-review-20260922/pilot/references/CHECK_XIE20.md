## C. Verdict and "Apo relative to" against task_common.md's tiers

**Agree with "Strong."** The paper reports two alternative states with quantitative CEST exchange data (83 probes total) and multiple independent lines of support required for "Strong" — inhibitor chemical-shift matching (PD173955→I1, imatinib→I2), stabilizing mutants that reproduce the CEST shifts and supply solved NMR structures for all three states, and a second method (2D-correlated spectra / magnetization-exchange cross peaks) — with every number traceable to Methods "CEST data fitting." The reference's own caveat that the functional link is weaker for I1 than I2 is itself stated by the paper's Discussion ("the biological significance of the I1 state is not apparent"), so it does not undercut the tier call. "Apo relative to" imatinib/PD173955 is also defensible: the paper's own Fig. 1E,F legends use the word "apo" specifically in comparison to PD173955-bound and imatinib-bound Abl, matching task_common.md's requirement to record apo relative to a specific ligand.
